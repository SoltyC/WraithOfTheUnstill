# Warden brief

Shadow of the Colossus practice [P]: design the body, the climbable surfaces, the battle flow and the arena **together**, as one document, and test each by playing. If ideas multiply, merge two into one. Items are never the key.

## The five axes

A new Warden must differ from **every** existing Warden on at least three axes.

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| Target | Static, structural, moving, hidden, aggressive, or vast? |
| Win condition | What does "released" require? (Break, open, steer, find, out-duel, reach.) |
| Discovery | What must the player *figure out*, and how does the world teach it? |
| Arena | Where, how big, and how does the fight reshape it? |
| Tempo and mood | Deliberate, staged, pursuit, stalking, frantic, epic; and the feeling it leaves. |

The current roster is the table in `docs/gameplay-design.md` §5. Update it whenever a Warden is designed.

## Every Warden brief must state

1. **Fiction:** what it held still, and why it isn't evil.
2. **The approach:** how terrain and landmarks lead to it (no marker needed), and the silhouette on the horizon.
3. **The waking:** the element lent at the waking (`cross-biome.md`), and what that element is used for in the fight.
4. **Phases:**
   - each with its telegraphs (honest, through material) and its valid solutions;
   - at least two, using known elements, so the open order holds;
   - a checkpoint at each phase boundary.
5. **Its calm moment:** where the fight breathes.
6. **The climb**, or the decision recorded for why there is none (BRIEF §8.4 quality gate).
7. **The release:** what changes in the biome, and the new play it opens (`gameplay-design.md` §7).
8. **What to measure:** time per phase, deaths per phase, which solutions were used.
