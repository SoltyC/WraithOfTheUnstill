The Long Walker (the dune Warden, D10)

Wake it, stumble it, climb on, steer it round the Stride to the camp's well. Stone and sand-surf come with its waking.

Follow the glassA Walker's footprint fused into the sand ahead. Press T until the compass tracks Follow the Glass, then go print to print to the star dune. The star duneOnly its court shows above the sand. Touch the brake stone in front of it (E) to wake it. Stumble itIt walks its circuit. A ring is pressed into the sand where each lifted foot will land: Ridge it (3, hold Q), Bloom a pit (3, F), or Frost the sand there (1, F). Then climb the kneeling leg and the plates up its side. Ride and steer itOn its back. Throw a Shard (3, tap Q) or a Gust (2, tap Q) at a glyph pillar to turn it toward that side. Brace against stone when it groans. Try steering it into the Stride. Bring it homeOn its back near the Wellhouse: it kneels by the gate. Turn the two mirror-stones on the roof (E) until the light runs mirror to mirror and down the well. After the releaseLying by the oasis, the erg restored.
