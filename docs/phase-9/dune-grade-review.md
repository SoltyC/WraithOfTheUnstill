## 1440p depth gates and revised strata

`tools/bot/dune-grade-gates.mjs` copies the actual depth32float target to a temporary readback (diagnostic only), adding no shader or late pipeline. RGB is cloned in the same animation-frame callback before asynchronous readback. Reverse-Z sky is excluded and the opaque mask is eroded 3 px to avoid TAA silhouette ambiguity. The mask includes props; a zero opaque-clipped count is stronger than the sand-only subset. Surface IDs are sampled beneath depth-reconstructed positions, at the bake's 4 m precision. Distance bins use actual GPU positions every 8 px, require dune weight >70%, and exclude positions outside the baked map. Half-pixel jitter and map boundaries remain approximate; these are not CPU/GPU collision parity measurements.

Wet regression: fixed foreground display luma restored **156.84 dry →118.18 wet** (24.7% darker), stilled **159.36 →121.51** (23.8% darker), with zero clipped opaque pixels. Different resolution/crop/grade means these are not interchangeable with the earlier D1a luma numbers. Rain/puddle were forced to zero to isolate wet absorption.

The sunset isolation retains bloom/grade on normal captures. Disabling only the LUT did not eliminate clipping; disabling bloom did. A hue-preserving soft shoulder in the existing LUT's spare pad.x is now blended by dune-camera weight; it leaves lower values and zero-dune frost/meadow arithmetic unchanged, and preserves headroom for grain. Both sunset states now have **zero opaque/sand/whole-frame clipped pixels** with bloom enabled. A regression verifies that leaving dunes clears the shoulder owner value. This does not change exposure or disable the accepted lighting path.

The 12 m sinusoidal sandstone pattern was still obvious at long range. It remained with real shadows disabled and disappeared when only the rock mask was zeroed, isolating it to the material. The accepted revision uses irregular neighbouring bed tones, sparse pale seams and restrained non-periodic fine variation, with footprint fade. The shared ledge pitch stays 12 m; no rebake, geometry or collision changes. Canyon sawteeth and coarse silhouettes remain explicit D7b/D2 defects.

The north range view has **15,457 sampled dune pixels at 2–4 km** in each of six noon/golden/night × stilled/restored views. All six have zero clipped opaque pixels/errors/late pipelines. It is now genuine range evidence rather than a camera-to-target estimate. It includes the cold-erg boundary and does not sign off the snow/sand transition or crest geometry. Canyon noon/dawn also has zero clipped opaque pixels/errors/late pipelines. Selected current 1280 px review images are in screenshots/dune-grade-gates, from verified 2560×1440 renders.

Fixed-scene performance (`tools/bot/dune-grade-perf.mjs`, perf.json): three 240-frame repeats each, scale 1, 27 timed passes. Close medians **15.042/15.062/15.073 ms**, mid **11.269/11.256/11.261 ms**, canyon **12.637/12.640/12.640 ms**; highest observed span **15.715 ms**. Zero GPU errors/late pipelines.
