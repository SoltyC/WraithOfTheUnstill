# D9b — Whirlers and Glass-sworn (in review)

The Whirler is a mobile caster whose tightening sand column warns of a volley and a travelling low dust devil; the Glass-sworn is an elite whose fused glass rejects Shard until thermal shock. These serve “learn by reading” and “the world remembers and answers”, feeding the 0–10 s combat loop with material decisions and the minute encounter loop with caster/elite pressure. The intended aesthetic is tension, followed by the release of a brittle body collapsing into its own material. Human enjoyment is NOT ASSESSED.

The caster keeps distance, gives a 1.05 s tightening warning, then fires three aimed sand clots and one bounded dust devil. Devils warn in place for 0.8 s, then travel at 4 m/s, write actual PLOUGH scars, last 5.3 s and hit only once each. Jumping clears the low whirl; a bend-step avoids its damage. A short warm screen veil retains silhouettes. Frost stops the column and clears its owned devils; a weapon breaks it. Gale Vortex is an optional second answer: reverses and pins its spin, interrupting the warning. No critical path needs Gale.

The elite uses the shared lunge/pack token behavior, fixed 120 health and 0.4 armour. Frost plus a weapon and Frost plus Shard are both answers. Shard immunity consumes the projectile without health loss or shove while warm; freezing removes that immunity. Its asymmetrical stone/glass body uses the existing two-bone legs and material chunk path. Fused glass currently uses thickness absorption, atmospheric reflection and facet highlights in the opaque shadow-casting pass. Full scene refraction is NOT ASSESSED and is not claimed.

Architecture: all eight creature slots remain fixed; the existing 12-shot pool carries an explicit material kind that resets to ice after each public shot. Six fixed devils reuse 96 additional records in the existing Shaped draw and its one per-frame chunk upload plus one shared powder-source uniform update. AI and posing live in duneCaster.js; new layouts in rigGlass.js; shaped.js remains below 800 lines. The dust veil uses the display uniform's unused w component after TAA, with menus composed independently. One fixed transparent powder draw (672 quads) reuses the accepted sand-puff fragment lighting/shadows; its shader is created at boot to warm before play. Pipelines, live graphics and allocations still need target verification.

Smell audit: no difficulty scaling, loot treadmill, fetch loop, hidden biome-element dependency or Warden reskin is introduced (clear by scope/code inspection). Material warnings are implemented but visibility at speed/night/storm is NOT ASSESSED. Frost is deliberately strong against glass; whether it becomes a dominant strategy across mixed encounters is NOT ASSESSED pending repeated play. Focus costs use existing verbs; starvation and time-to-clear are pending. Bare-core fun, meaningful world placement/reward, natural route pacing and human feel are NOT ASSESSED; D11 will place mixed-role fights around sites. Scope is bounded to two archetypes, shared rendering and six transient whirls.

Initial checks: seven focused regressions pass, including real Stone hit ownership, frozen staff damage, a combat Vortex, frozen spiral/debris hand-off, bounded eight-body simulation and short sand-shot veil. They exposed and fixed a shared bug: frozen armoured targets were still divided through the warm-armour compensation path with zero pierce, yielding zero ordinary weapon damage; the existing den mother now has a regression too. Full suite: 785 tests pass; locked production build passes. Target-GPU captures and bot fights are pending; this is not a completion note.
