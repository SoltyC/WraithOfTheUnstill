The world is stilling. The wind has died in the meadows, the dunes have stopped walking, the fen lies like glass. What was left of its motion gathered in an empty robe. That is you.

The ground remembers.

Every step, every spell, every creature writes into one shared land of snow, sand, mud and grass, and the world carries the marks until it slowly heals them.

Draw in the snow

I · The frost steppe

Carve the snow at speed.

Surf the drifts down from the monastery, throw a ribbon of ice, raise a spire. The snow keeps every line you cut.

II · The highland meadow

Bring back the wind.

Grass that parts around you, a village of veiled pilgrims, a manor that is not what it seems. Glide from the ridges on the robe.

III · The glass dunes

Ride the sand.

An erg that has stopped walking, storms that arrive like walls, ruins half-buried in amber light. Raise stone from the sand and surf its crests.

IV · The mirefen

Skim the still water.

A fen like a mirror at dawn. Everything out here listens, and the water tells on you.

Not killed.
Released.

In each land a colossal Warden holds its element still. You don't slay them. You find out what each one is keeping from happening, and you set it free. Motion returns to the land, and it is yours.
